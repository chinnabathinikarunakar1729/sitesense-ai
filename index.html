How it works

From camera feed to warning

Three steps, running continuously across every site in a portfolio.

01

Capture

Existing site cameras stream footage in — no new hardware to install or manage.

02

Analyze

A bank of purpose-built models checks each frame for fall risks, missing PPE, fire hazards, and structural conditions — in parallel, all the time.

03

Alert

Safety officers and site managers get the hazard the moment it's seen, with a snapshot — instead of finding out after someone's hurt.

What it watches for

A dedicated model for each hazard

Not one general-purpose detector — a model trained for each specific risk, so each one is sharper than a jack-of-all-trades system would be.

Falls & heights

Guardrails, open edges, unsecured workers at height, and ladder or scaffold setups that aren't to code.
